fix(ai): correct data outputs treated as errors and relax command tag parsing

- `useChat.ts`: Fixed a critical bug where read commands (`get_sheet_data`, `get_range_csv`, etc.) were pushing their data into the `errors` array of the execution result. This caused the UI to incorrectly display a warning, and worse, caused the AI to think its read operations were failing, leading to confused, looping behavior. Extracted read results into a dedicated `outputs` array.
- `useChat.ts`: Relaxed the regex that parses `<EXCEL_CMD>` tags to also match `EXCEL_CMD>` and `CEL_CMD>`. Certain models (like Nemotron) would occasionally drop the leading `<` when formatting their output, causing the entire command execution engine to blindly ignore their commands. This ensures commands execute even with minor formatting hallucinations.
